# Projection is the latest Curia poll, then overhangs

The headline Projection allocates 120 seats from the latest Taxpayers’ Union–Curia poll among parties at or over 5% plus Te Pāti Māori, using largest remainder, then adds overhangs. Blending unrelated pollsters was rejected: house effects are large and Dieuwe will republish when a new Curia lands. National is assumed to hold every 2026-notional National electorate. On the 1–4 Aug 2026 poll that is 40 of 120 against a haul of 45, so five seats are the National overhang. The Transfer slider then adds a tactical overhang for each 2023 Labour or Green seat that is winnable at that share of the NZ First + ACT candidate pool. NZ First and ACT stay on the poll allocation. House size and the Coalition total move with that slider.
