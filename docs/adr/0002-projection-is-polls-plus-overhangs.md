# Projection is the latest Curia poll, then overhangs

The headline Projection allocates 120 seats from the latest Taxpayers’ Union–Curia poll among parties at or over 5% plus Te Pāti Māori, using largest remainder, then adds overhangs. Blending unrelated pollsters was rejected: house effects are large and Dieuwe will republish when a new Curia lands. Switch, if any, reallocates that poll before the 120. National’s headline number is electorate seats: every seat National currently holds, plus each 2023 Labour or Green seat winnable at the Transfer. Overhang is one number: those electorates minus National’s share of the 120. House size and the Coalition total move with either slider.
