# Switch splits by partner poll ratio

The Switch slider moves a share of the current National party-vote Poll onto NZ First and ACT, then re-runs the 120-seat allocation. The page does not pick a partner, so the moved slice is split in those two parties’ current poll ratio — not 50/50, and not a third slider. Transfer still does not move party votes; Switch still does not move electorate votes. Default is 10%, same as Transfer: one in ten Coalition voters splitting both ways.
